Privacy Policy — Sudoku

Last updated: October 19, 2026

This policy describes what data the Sudoku app ("the app"), developed by Paulo Carpinetti, collects, how it is used and how you can request its deletion.

Summary

  • You can play entirely without an account. Your progress, statistics and preferences are saved on your device.
  • With or without an account, the app uses Google services to show ads, measure app usage and report technical crashes (see the sections below).
  • If you create an account (optional), your game data is stored in the cloud, and your player name and best scores appear in the ranking, visible to other players with an account.
  • You can delete your account and all associated data at any time.

Using the app without an account

You can play the app without ever creating an account. In that case, game progress, statistics and preferences are saved only on your device, and we do not send this game data to the cloud. Even without an account, the app sends technical and advertising data, described in the "Ads", "Usage measurement" and "Crash reports" sections.

Data collected when you create an account (optional)

Creating an account is always optional. If you choose to create an account (with email/password or by signing in with your Google Account, via Google Play Games Services), we collect and store in the cloud:

  • Account identifier and email — used for authentication (Firebase Authentication, by Google).
  • Player name — a unique name you choose, registered so that no two players have the same name.
  • Progress of the game in progress (if any), so you can continue where you left off on another device.
  • Game statistics — games played, wins, losses, best scores per difficulty, win streak.
  • App preferences — theme, timer display, font size and language.

This data is stored in Cloud Firestore (also by Google) and used to sync your game experience across devices signed in to the same account and for the ranking described below. We do not use this data for advertising, we do not sell it and we do not share it with third parties other than the infrastructure provider (Google Firebase), which is required for the service to work.

Player ranking

If you have an account, your best scores per difficulty, your win streak, your number of wins and your player name are published in a ranking. This information can be seen by other players who also have an account. Your email does not appear in the ranking. Players without an account do not take part in the ranking. When you delete your account, your ranking entry and your player name are deleted.

Ads

The app shows ads through Google AdMob, including the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) for consent to personalized ads. To serve and measure ads, AdMob may collect:

  • Advertising ID of the device (a resettable identifier provided by the Android system).
  • App usage data related to showing and interacting with ads, used for personalization (when consented) and performance measurement.

Before showing any ad, we offer players in regions that require this choice (such as the EEA and the UK) the option to consent to or refuse personalized ads, through the UMP. Ads are not personalized when consent is refused or not yet obtained. You can also reset or delete the advertising ID in the Android settings. Google acts as a processor of this data under its own privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy).

Usage measurement

The app uses Firebase Analytics, by Google, to understand in aggregate how the app is used and to improve it. This applies to all players, with or without an account. The service may collect app usage data (such as app opens and screens visited), device information (model, Android version, language and country) and an identifier of the app installation. We do not use this data to identify you personally.

Crash reports

To fix errors, the app uses Firebase Crashlytics, by Google. When the app has a technical crash, it automatically sends, for all players, with or without an account, a report with technical information: device model, Android version, app version, device state at the time of the crash (such as available memory) and the technical log of the error. These reports do not contain your games, statistics, email or player name, and are used only to diagnose and fix problems.

Device permissions

The app uses internet access and connection state, needed for ads, usage measurement, crash reports and, when you have an account, authentication and cloud sync. The app also declares access to the advertising ID, used by ads. No other sensitive permission (location, camera, microphone, contacts, storage, etc.) is requested or used.

Account and data deletion

You can permanently delete your account and all personal data associated with it at any time, in two ways. Deletion removes your identifier and email, your player name, your progress, your statistics, your preferences and your ranking entry:

  • In the app: Menu → Options → Account → "Delete account" (with explicit confirmation).
  • Outside the app, including if you have already uninstalled it: visit our deletion request page or send an email to pauloscarpinetti@gmail.com.

Requests through the external route are handled within 30 days. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone; progress, statistics and preferences saved only locally on the device are not affected. Ad, usage measurement and crash report data is kept by Google according to its own retention periods and is not linked to your account in the app.

Data retention

Account data is stored for as long as your account exists, or until you request deletion through either of the routes above.

Your rights

You can request access to, correction of or deletion of your data at any time by contacting us at the email below.

Children

The app is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from this age group. If a parent or guardian finds that a child has created an account, they can request deletion through the email below or the deletion page, and we will delete the data.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in the app. The date of the last update is always shown at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy or about your data: pauloscarpinetti@gmail.com
