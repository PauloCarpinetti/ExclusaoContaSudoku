Delete account and data — Sudoku

This page lets you request the permanent deletion of your account and all personal data associated with it in the Sudoku app, even if you have already uninstalled the app.

Warning: this action cannot be undone. All progress saved in the cloud, game statistics and synced preferences will be permanently deleted. Progress saved only on your device is not affected by this request.

What will be deleted

  • Your sign-in account (Google sign-in and/or email and password)
  • Game statistics saved in the cloud (games played, wins, losses, best scores)
  • Game progress saved in the cloud (if any)
  • Synced preferences (theme, timer display, etc.)

How to request

Send an email to pauloscarpinetti@gmail.com from the same address associated with your account (your sign-in email, or the email of your Google account), with the subject "Account deletion — Sudoku", asking for your account and data to be deleted.

We confirm the request and your identity (by checking that the sender address matches the email registered on the account) and process the deletion within 30 days. You will receive a confirmation by email when the deletion is complete.

In-app alternative

If you still have the app installed and can sign in to your account, the fastest way to delete your account and data is directly in the app: Menu → Options → Account → "Delete account".
